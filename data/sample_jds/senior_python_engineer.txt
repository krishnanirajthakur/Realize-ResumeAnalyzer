Job Title: Senior Python & Machine Learning Engineer

Company: Nexus Data Labs
Location: Remote / Hybrid

Role Overview:
Nexus Data Labs is seeking a seasoned Senior Python & Machine Learning Engineer to design, build, and optimize our core predictive intelligence platforms and scalable backend microservices. You will work closely with product and data science teams to architect production-ready data pipelines and deterministic machine learning services.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, implement, and maintain high-throughput backend APIs using Python, FastAPI, and PostgreSQL.
- Build classical machine learning pipelines, feature engineering workflows, and text analytics using Scikit-Learn, Pandas, NumPy, and NLTK.
- Containerize services with Docker and orchestrate resilient deployments on Kubernetes and AWS cloud infrastructure.
- Establish automated CI/CD deployment pipelines with GitHub Actions and maintain comprehensive unit testing with pytest.
- Collaborate in Agile Scrum sprints with cross-functional engineering teams and track tasks in Jira.

Minimum Qualifications (Required):
- At least 5 years of professional software engineering experience.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related technical STEM discipline.
- Proficient in Python, SQL, and database design with PostgreSQL.
- Hands-on experience with classical Machine Learning and NLP (Scikit-Learn, Pandas, NumPy, TF-IDF).
- Production experience with Docker and Linux systems.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice to Have):
- Experience with Kubernetes, AWS, or Terraform.
- Familiarity with FastAPI, Redis, or Apache Kafka.
- Experience with Unit Testing (pytest) and CI/CD pipelines.
- Master's degree in Computer Science or Data Science.
